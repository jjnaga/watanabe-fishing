# Watanabe Fishing - Spec

## What Is This?

A landing page for **Watanabe Fishing** - a fishing experience business run by Jay (the user's buddy) in Hawaii.

## The Offering

- **Duration:** 4 hours
- **Price:** $99 per person
- **Target:** Complete beginners who've never fished and want to try
- **Includes:** All gear/tools + light refreshments
- **Type:** Shore fishing (likely, needs confirmation)

## Questions Still Open

Before building, we need answers to:

1. **Location** - Which Hawaiian island? (Ulua = Giant Trevally suggests Hawaii)
2. **Booking flow** - Phone? Text? External booking system? Contact form?
3. **Jay's vibe** - Chill local uncle? Experienced guide? Teaching-focused?
4. **Colors/branding** - Any preferences or free to pick?
5. **Instagram** - Does Jay have one to link?
6. **Photos** - Real photos available or using placeholders?
7. **Shore or boat?** - Affects imagery and expectations
8. **Group size** - Max people? Solo bookings ok?
9. **Restrictions** - Age minimum? Physical requirements?

## Page Structure

### Hero Section
- Big full-width image of Jay with an ulua (mean pose)
- Headline + subheadline
- CTA button (Book Now / Get Started)

### Navigation (sticky header)
- Logo/Name
- Anchor links: About, What's Included, Testimonials, Book

### About Section
- Who is Jay
- The experience philosophy
- Why beginners love it

### What's Included Section
- 4 hours of fishing
- All gear provided
- Light refreshments
- Expert guidance
- (Maybe) What you'll learn

### Testimonials Section
- 3-4 mock testimonials
- Names, short quotes, maybe star ratings
- Keep them believable (not over the top)

### Book/Contact Section
- Clear CTA
- Booking method (TBD)
- Contact info

### Footer
- Simple: copyright, maybe social links

## Vibes & Intent

**Feel:** Local, authentic, welcoming, not corporate. Like getting a recommendation from a friend.

**Not:** Cheesy tourist trap, stock photo hell, over-designed, complicated.

**Tone:** Friendly, casual, confident. Jay knows what he's doing and wants to share the experience.

**Trust signals:** Real testimonials (even if mock for now), clear pricing, no hidden stuff.

## Success Criteria

A professional developer should look at this and think:
- "This is a real site"
- "A pro made this"
- "Clean, not too much info, just right"
- "I'd actually book this"
